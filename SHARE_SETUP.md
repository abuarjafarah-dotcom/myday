# Share to My Day (iPhone share sheet)

Send any text, link or email to the dashboard from the Share button. It uses the same capture address as the back-tap
shortcut, so items are sorted the same way (tasks, grocery by store, ideas).

## Set up (about 5 minutes, once)

1. Open **Shortcuts**, press and hold your back-tap capture shortcut, tap **Duplicate**. Rename the copy **Send to My Day**.
2. Open the copy. Delete the **Dictate Text** step.
3. Tap the **ⓘ** at the bottom, turn on **Show in Share Sheet**. Under "Receive", keep **Text**, **URLs** and **Safari web pages**.
4. In **Get Contents of URL**, change the `text` field from *Dictated Text* to **Shortcut Input**.
5. Leave the rest as it is (the reminder steps and the notification).

## Use it

- In Mail, Safari, Notes or Messages: select text (or just tap **Share**) → **Send to My Day**.
- A notification says what was added. On the dashboard the "From your voice note" card has **Undo**.

Tip: start the text with a store name to file groceries ("Costco: milk, eggs"), or with "Idea:" for the ideas list.
